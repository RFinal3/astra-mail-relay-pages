Astra Mail Relay Privacy Policy

Purpose and access. Astra Mail Relay is a personal-use application operated by Ryan. With the account owner’s authorization, it can search and read Gmail messages and create drafts. It requests Gmail read-only and compose permissions. Although the compose permission is broader, the relay currently exposes draft creation, not sending.

Use and sharing. Email data is used to respond to the account owner’s requests. When Astra reads a message, its content may be included in Astra’s context and transmitted to the remote AI provider through OpenRouter. No bulk mailbox synchronization or background scanning is intended by the relay.

Storage. The relay stores authorization credentials on the owner’s computer and may store draft identifiers and content fingerprints locally. Drafts created through the relay are stored in Gmail. Astra’s conversation history and memory are stored locally on the owner’s machine. Remote AI providers may process the information included in requests; their retention practices are governed by their applicable terms and settings.

Training settings. The operator has configured no-training settings for data, prompts, and responses. These are configuration choices, not an independent guarantee of a provider’s practices.

Revoking access. The account owner can revoke the app’s access through their Google Account’s third-party access settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete drafts already in Gmail or existing local records.

Contact. Questions about this personal application can be directed to the support email listed on its Google authorization screen.
