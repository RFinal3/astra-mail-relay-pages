Astra Mail Relay

Astra Mail Relay is a personal tool that connects Ryan’s Astra assistant to a Gmail account used for data-work activities. It lets Astra search and read authorized email and prepare drafts for Ryan to review. The relay does not provide an email-sending function.

Astra uses a remote AI service to process conversation context. When Astra reads an email, its content may be included in that context and sent to the AI service. More detail is available in the privacy policy.
